The house

A corner market that still cuts your meat.

Hilltop Market is a family-run grocery and butcher in Otay, Chula Vista. People come for carne asada, house guacamole, and tortillas that are still warm in the cooler. They stay because someone at the counter already knows their order.

It is a small store packed tight — produce on the left, the meat case glowing on the right, liquor stacked to the ceiling behind the register. SNAP/EBT and cards are fine. The lot is right there. Thank you, we appreciate your business.
